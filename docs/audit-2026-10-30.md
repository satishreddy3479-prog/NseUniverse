# Audit of 30 Oct 2026: Strategy builder, Intraday analysis, Prediction, Connection diagnostics

Three lenses: **data engineer** (provenance, time, missing values), **ML engineer** (leakage, evaluation, forward test, logging),
**quant** (formulas, assumptions, consistency). Rules applied: [financial-data](../.claude/rules/financial-data.md) and CLAUDE.md
(no fabricated data, missing stays missing, flag stale, timestamps kept apart, assumptions labelled).

## How the audit was done and what it does not cover
- Searched every file of the four sections (frontend pages, `lib/strategy.ts`, `lib/optionModel.ts`, `components/CommandCenter.tsx`, `viz.tsx`, `Prediction*.tsx`, `Diagnostics.tsx`; backend `intraday/*`, `prediction/*`, `domain/session.py`, status routes) for: `?? 0` and `|| 0` zero-fills, numeric literals and assumed constants, random, mock and demo values, silent `except`, fallbacks that make up a value, and "all clear" states shown when a request failed.
- Read the decision paths in full: payoff and Greeks, forward and volatility, session clock, ledger and settling, the nightly dataset, the evaluation gate, the status routes.
- Ran the backend (535+) and frontend (314) tests.
- **Not done:** a statement-by-statement read of all ~15,000 lines. Display-only styling, and parts of the backend breadth and futures code (`market/*`), were not read line by line. Live SSH checks of the server were done by the owner (see the server report), not by Claude.

## Verified correct (no change)
- **Payoff and risk:** exact piecewise-linear at expiry, kinks at strikes, unlimited sides from the slope above the last strike, breakevens by interpolation. Hand-calculated tests for every template.
- **Templates:** strikes come from the listed chain by position (ATM ± n listed strikes), never by an assumed step.
- **Lot size:** read from the stored chain; the builder refuses to show rupee P&L without it.
- **Black-76 and Greeks:** the TypeScript implementation is tested against vectors generated by the backend `volatility.py`, against finite differences, and for delta and gamma parity.
- **Forward:** read from put-call parity in the stored chain (median of the five strikes nearest spot, spread shown). If fewer than three strikes have both prices the page says so and falls back to spot grown at the stated rate.
- **Sigma levels:** spot × (1 ± k × ATM IV × √T), labelled as the expected-move convention; the probability of profit is a separate lognormal and says so.
- **Prediction:** only series with verified timing feed the models (`timing_verified`, `pit.py`); tuning and holdout are separate; the ledger is append-only (triggers and content hash); today's 8 forecasts were recorded inside the window.
- **Intraday ML:** features use samples at or before t and labels only later ones; rows are written after the close and never updated; the gate counts sessions; no probability is shown without it; the sentiment is rules-based and labelled so.
- **Missing values:** stored as null, shown as "—"; sums skip and count missing contracts.

## Open items (not fixed here; need a decision or more data)
1. **Two implementations of IV and Greeks (TypeScript and Python).** They share the formula but differ in conventions: the backend forward uses parity at one strike (the frontend: median of five); backend delta is with respect to the forward and discounted, the frontend delta is with respect to spot; backend theta is the analytic derivative, the frontend theta the one-day change. Differences are small after fix 1 but exist. Decide one convention and share vectors in both test suites.
2. **Assumed constants that are labelled but not sourced:** discount rate 6 % (`config.risk_free_rate`, the Strategy builder setting), trading-cost model (brokerage 20, STT 0.05 %, exchange 0.00183 %, SEBI, stamp, GST 18 %, slippage 0.5 point) and `cost_lot_size = 65`. They are shown as assumptions. The owner should replace them with the broker contract note, and the paper-trading lot size should come from the stored chain like everywhere else.
3. **Provisional model constants:** the sentiment weights and scales, the lean band of 3 points, the setup entry cutoff 15:00 and the barrier `k` are judgement calls, not fitted. The docs say so; they must be evaluated on stored sessions before anyone calls them meaningful. Only 1 labelled intraday session exists.
4. **Expiry end time** is the session close (15:40) for every expiry (`SESSION_CLOSE_MIN`); if NSE removes the CAS extension next month, change `lib/session.ts` and `domain/session.py` together. The claim that it will be removed is the owner's and is unverified.
5. **Exchange holidays** are known to the backend calendar but not to the browser: a manual fetch on a weekday holiday during 09:15 to 15:40 would count as a session sample in the charts.
6. **Closed and saved strategies** live in the browser (localStorage): not backed up, not shared between devices, lost if the browser data is cleared.
7. **Forward test evidence is empty:** nothing has resolved yet (first result after the 30 Oct close). The settle and log path is covered by tests, not yet by a live example.
8. **Backups:** the server's own timer runs (two dated folders on 30 Oct), the observation database copy and an off-server copy are still to be confirmed or set up (see the storage plan).
