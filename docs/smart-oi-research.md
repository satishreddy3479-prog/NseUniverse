# Smart OI divergence indicators: research on stored sessions

Generated by `frontend/src/test/smart-oi-research.test.ts` from 7 stored sessions (2026-11-09, 2026-11-13, 2026-11-16, 2026-11-17, 2026-11-18, 2026-11-19, 2026-11-20). The first 5 sessions are the fitting set, the last 2 the judging set: no threshold was tuned on the judging set. Signals are taken at the candle they are confirmed on, without looking at what happened after; a flow divergence that later fails still counts as a signal.

Read-only measurement. A signal is judged by the move of the NIFTY close after it (forward return) against the same move after every candle of the same sessions (the baseline). The hit rate of a bullish signal is the share of cases where price was higher after the horizon; of a bearish signal, lower. The interval is a Wilson 95 % interval. **Verdict** needs at least 30 judging-set cases and the whole interval above the baseline hit rate; otherwise the indicator stays "not validated".

## What this does and does not show

- 7 sessions is a small sample: most indicators have too few cases to be judged, and the verdict says so instead of rounding up. More sessions are stored every day; re-run this after each week.
- Cases inside one session are not independent (a trending day gives many similar signals): the intervals are narrower than the real uncertainty.
- The forward move is measured on the NIFTY close of the candle grid, before costs. Nothing here is an order, a target or a recommendation.
- Neutral signals (absorption, unusual Smart OI) have no direction and are not judged here.
